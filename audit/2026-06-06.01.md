# zkCoins Specification — Logical Consistency Audit

**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Snapshot:** [`zk-coins/docs@527df18`](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/commit/527df18) — *"Spec: drop Commitment-SMT/MMR for an on-chain nullifier set"* ([#21](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/pull/21)), which landed during this audit and touched all 7 normative chapters.
**Scope:** the entire normative specification (`docs/specification/*.md`, 7 chapters), the architecture deep-dives (`docs/architecture/*.md`, 13 files), and the risk register (`docs/risks.md`).
**Goal:** logical consistency, completeness, contradictions, and underspecification. **Not** prose/style. **Not** implementation review. Design-level only, independent of code.

## Method

- The seven normative `specification/` chapters (`index`, `foundations`, `proofs`, `onchain`, `transport-recovery`, `access-explorer`, `architecture`) were read in full by the auditor for cross-chapter consistency.
- The thirteen `architecture/` deep-dives were reviewed by two delegated sub-auditors in two clusters (crypto/identity; privacy/trust/flows). Their findings were filtered against the normative spec — several apparent contradictions were briefing artefacts (e.g. `address = SHA-256(Pk₀)` is *correct* per [foundations §1.1](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#11-cryptographic-primitives), where `H = SHA-256`).
- `docs/risks.md` was read to calibrate severity and to avoid re-reporting already-acknowledged limitations.

The spec was actively being edited during the audit: commit #21 landed mid-pass. All findings below were re-validated against the post-#21 working tree.

## Headline

The normative `specification/` is substantial, internally largely consistent, and is being actively hardened. Commit #21 is strong evidence: it **resolves exactly one of this audit's top normative findings** (the off-chain aggregate-proof availability gap) and a related blind-scanner / address-keying contradiction that the audit was independently converging on. Three classes remain:

1. **Five concrete normative findings** — `medium` to `low`, mostly localised.
2. **One large doc-set consistency issue** — the `architecture/` deep-dives are partly stale or aspirational and contradict the normative spec on load-bearing points. **Largest actionable item.**
3. **One implication for `zk-coins/research`**: [PR #3](https://github.com/zk-coins/research/pull/3) (`NullifierChaining.tla`) modelled the per-batch `prev_root → post_root` chaining that #21 removed. The claim it models (no double-spend) survives; the mechanism changes. The TLA+ will become **simpler**, not larger.

## What #21 already fixed (validates the audit)

| Issue | Status |
|---|---|
| **Off-chain aggregate validity proof had no availability guarantee** (old `onchain.md` §3.6/§3.7) AND tension with §3.4 (*"publisher never holds a proof"*) — the trustless double-spend check depended on a fetchable proof with no assigned producer or replication. | **Resolved by #21.** Commit message verbatim: *"…Requirement 3/4 gap"*. New design publishes spent nullifiers in the clear on-chain; every node rebuilds the accumulator first-spend-wins from Bitcoin alone. |
| **Address-keyed scan in a blind scanner.** Old `onchain.md` §3.6 step 5 required scanners to key Commitment-SMT leaves by the spender's `address`, but `ProofData` carried none, and §1.2 keeps `Pkᵢ` rotating and unlinkable. The rebuildable global root was therefore not a pure function of chain data without leaking the address. | **Resolved by #21.** Commitment-SMT/MMR removed; [foundations §1.6](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#16-trees-one-global-structure-one-per-account-structure) now states the impossibility explicitly (*"either rebuildable from the chain… or privacy-preserving — never both"*). |

## A · Remaining normative findings

| ID | Severity | Finding | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | Medium | **`DetectTag` domain-separation reuses one context for two derivations.** [`foundations.md` §1.3](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#13-per-coin-keys-note-encryption--detection) defines both `dk = HKDF("zkCoins/v1/DetectTag", ivk)` **and** `detect_tag = Hc("zkCoins/v1/DetectTag", dk ‖ epk)`. [`foundations.md` §1.1](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#11-cryptographic-primitives) states *"Reusing a tag for two purposes is forbidden."* Different primitives (HKDF vs Poseidon) make a practical collision unlikely, but the rule is violated. **Fix:** split tags, e.g. `"zkCoins/v1/DetectKey"` for the HKDF and `"zkCoins/v1/DetectTag"` for the Poseidon hash. | foundations §1.1 + §1.3 |
| **F2** | Medium | **Detection model is internally inconsistent.** §1.3 claims both (a) relay-side efficiency (*"without trial-decrypting everything"*) and (b) *"deterministic tags are linkable by a relay that stores them."* The mechanism specified in [`transport-recovery.md` §4.4](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/transport-recovery.md#44-note-discovery) (recipient locally recomputes `Hc(dk‖epk)` for each candidate `epk`) gives **neither**: it is a local O(n) scan, invisible to the relay. [`transport-recovery.md` §4.7](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/transport-recovery.md#47-metadata-and-privacy-tradeoffs) more accurately locates the linkability at **pull** time (recipient hands a tag set to a serving node), not store time. **Fix:** reconcile §1.3 with §4.7; clarify exactly what a foreign relay can correlate. | foundations §1.3 vs transport-recovery §4.4/§4.7 |
| **F3** | Medium | **Issuer↔asset linkability is not disclosed.** [`foundations.md` §1.4](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#14-identifiers-and-hashes) defines `asset_id = Hc("AssetId", genesis_tag ‖ Pk₀ ‖ name_hash ‖ decimals)`, committing to the issuer's `Pk₀`. Per [`foundations.md` §1.2](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#12-key-hierarchy), `Pk₀` *"appears on-chain only in the first transition"* of an account, and `address = H(Pk₀)`. Any asset holder knows `name_hash` and `decimals` (from `IssuanceTerms` / bundle) and can, by testing on-chain `Pkᵢ` candidates, recover `Pk₀` → identify the issuer's account/address. Issuer anonymity is therefore not provided. The Requirement-2 privacy claim covers *"amounts, sender, receiver, graph"* — issuer identity is not enumerated. **Fix:** either disclose this explicitly in the privacy section, or change `asset_id` derivation to break the link (e.g., a per-asset issuance key separate from `Pk₀`). | foundations §1.2 + §1.4; architecture §6.5 |
| **F4** | Low | **Coin-history-SMT label contradicts its use.** [`foundations.md` §1.6](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#16-trees-one-global-structure-one-per-account-structure) labels the coin-history SMT *"for in-circuit **non-inclusion**"*, but [`proofs.md` §2.1 clause 2b](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/proofs.md#21-the-compliance-predicate) uses it for **inclusion** of the input coin (membership of the coin in the prior coin-history root). Likely a stale terminology; clarify whether the structure serves inclusion, non-inclusion, or both. | foundations §1.6 vs proofs §2.1 |
| **F5** | Low / Note | **Static account-level `nk` — blast radius.** Per [`foundations.md` §1.2](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#12-key-hierarchy), `nk` is *"account-level"* and is not rotated. Outside-unlinkability is unaffected, but compromise of `nk` makes every nullifier of the account back-computable to its `coin.identifier` by anyone who knows the identifiers. `nk` deserves the same hardening posture as the spend keys; it should be listed as a high-value secret with that posture explicit. | foundations §1.2 |

## B · New honest tradeoffs introduced by #21 (not findings — explicit by design)

- **Input-count leak per spend.** Publishing nullifiers in the clear exposes `kⱼ` per record (and `kⱼ = 0` marks a mint). [`onchain.md` §3.5](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/onchain.md#35-inscription-format) labels this *"an accepted, bounded leak outside Requirement 2"* and offers optional padding. Cleanly handled.
- **Unprunable accumulator + no light-client shortcut.** [`onchain.md` §3.7](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/onchain.md#37-the-nullifier-accumulator) is explicit: non-membership has no compact root shortcut; a verifier maintains the accumulator or delegates to ≥1-honest-of-many nodes. *"…standing cost… shared with the Shielded CSV paper and with Zcash, not specific to zkCoins."* Honest.

## C · Doc-set consistency (top actionable)

The `architecture/` deep-dives lag the normative `specification/` and contradict it on load-bearing points. A reader landing there gets false information.

| Deep-dive contradiction | Concrete divergence from normative spec |
|---|---|
| `architecture/proof-system.md` L28 | *"Verify all incoming coins (Schnorr signatures, Merkle inclusion)"* — per-coin signatures. Spec: **one** transition signature, no per-coin keys ([`proofs.md` §2.1 clause 2](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/proofs.md#21-the-compliance-predicate)). |
| `architecture/information-model.md` | `AssetId` uses raw `name` instead of `name_hash`; the catalog calls itself "complete" but omits `nk` and the nullifier derivation entirely. |
| `architecture/key-management.md` | HD derivation path differs from `m/1798'/account'` ([`foundations.md` §1.2](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#12-key-hierarchy)). |
| `architecture/aliasing.md` | v0.2 design with a single static `account_pub`; conflicts with the rotating `Pkᵢ` of §1.2 and uses an incompatible HD path. |
| `architecture/privacy-model.md` vs `information-flow.md` vs `transaction-flow.md` | Three different node-state stories (stateful / "stateless" / single shared backend). The normative spec ([`architecture.md` §6.1](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/architecture.md#61-components-and-responsibilities) + [`transport-recovery.md` §4.6](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/transport-recovery.md#46-data-availability--replication-factor-k)) is unambiguous: stateful, with a k=3 bundle store. |
| Several deep-dives + parts of `risks.md` | *"Trustless receive"* discussed in the present tense as not yet implemented. The spec ([`proofs.md` §2.3.3](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/proofs.md#233-receive) + [`onchain.md` §3.7](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/onchain.md#37-the-nullifier-accumulator)) specifies it as the target. The doc set lacks a present-vs-target status convention. |

**Recommendation:** update the deep-dives to the normative state, or clearly mark them as historical rationale, or replace them with the `specification/` chapters plus `risks.md` for implementation status.

## D · What the spec gets right (balance)

- **Custody safety vs availability cleanly separated.** [`transport-recovery.md` §4.6](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/transport-recovery.md#46-data-availability--replication-factor-k): *"Custody safety MUST NOT depend on availability."*
- **`block_anchor` bounds.** [`onchain.md` §3.5](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/onchain.md#35-inscription-format) bounds forward and stale anchoring (≤100 blocks).
- **Issuance circularity avoided.** [`architecture.md` §6.5](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/architecture.md#65-issuance--trustless-permissionless-emission) evaluates the validity-window check against `block_anchor.height`, not the (unknown) inclusion height.
- **Honest privacy boundary.** [`architecture.md` §6.6](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/architecture.md#66-threat-model-and-trust-configurations): *"the node sees your plaintext, but you are the operator, so nothing leaks"* — privacy is from on-chain observers and uninvolved third parties, not from the chosen prover-node.
- **Capability model.** [`access-explorer.md`](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/access-explorer.md) — ownership proof, view grant, per-coin `K_tx`, balance attestation: cryptographically consistent across the spectrum.
- **Digest size pinned (new in #21).** [`foundations.md` §1.7](https://github.com/zk-coins/docs/blob/develop/docs/specification/foundations.md#17-encoding-rules): *"Every `Hc` value used as identifier/nullifier/root is a full 256-bit Poseidon digest… a single 64-bit Goldilocks element MUST NOT be used"* — important precision.
- **Cross-chapter symbols line up.** Domain tags, HRPs (`zk`/`zkgrant`/`zkview`/`zkavk`), and identifier derivations are mutually consistent across all seven normative chapters.

## E · Already in `risks.md` (not re-reported)

Backend visibility over plaintext (own node case), data availability vs proof loss, coin-creation-time leak + same-tx output linkability, BitVM bridge currently federated, anonymity-set / network effect, receiver reachability. The audit credits these as acknowledged; they are out of scope for new findings.

## F · Implication for `zk-coins/research`

[`research#3`](https://github.com/zk-coins/research/pull/3) (`formal/nullifier-chaining/NullifierChaining.tla`) modelled the per-batch `prev_root → post_root` chaining that #21 removed. The property it checks (`NoDoubleSpend` over the finalised ledger) remains the right claim, but the mechanism it encodes (`Chains(ts, baseAcc)` enforcing chained non-membership and insertion) no longer matches the spec. The replacement design is simpler:

> *Canonical total order over Bitcoin-published `SpendRecord`s; for each `nf` published, the first occurrence wins; any later record reusing it is invalid.*

The updated model removes claimed `prevRoot`/`postRoot` per transition and removes the explicit chaining predicate, in favour of: an append-only `chain` of records (each carrying its `nf` set in the clear), `acc = union over chain of records' nfs`, and an acceptance gate that admits a record only if its `nf`s are disjoint from the current `acc`. The `NoDoubleSpend` invariant is unchanged. The TLA+ gets shorter.

## Recommendations — priority order

1. **F1** — split the `DetectTag` context strings. Smallest change; removes a self-rule violation.
2. **F3** — disclose issuer-↔-asset linkability, or break it. Expectation management for permissionless issuance.
3. **C** — reconcile the `architecture/` deep-dives with the normative spec. Largest leverage for external readers / future implementers.
4. **F2 / F4** — terminology and detection-model passes.
5. **research#3** — refactor onto the new first-spend-wins design.

## Out-of-scope

This audit does not cover: prose/style, the implementation (`zk-coins/node`), the SDK or app, or formal-cryptographic security proofs (knowledge-soundness of the proof system, NIP-44 confidentiality, BIP-340 unforgeability — all assumed by the protocol). Recovery-from-seed under partial network availability is treated as a liveness property per the spec; no quantitative availability analysis is included.
