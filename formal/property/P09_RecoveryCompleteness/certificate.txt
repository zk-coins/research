                correctness/safety + availability/liveness).
Claim         : Two halves, graded to mirror the Pass-3 audit split.

                  (i)   NO FALSE-ACCEPT -- the recovered set contains no forged
                        or otherwise unverifiable bundle. Every member passed
                        the spec Sec. 4.5 step-4 gate: its recursive per-account
                        proof verifies against Bitcoin (A1 KnowledgeSound) AND
                        the wallet's own seed-derived keys can read it.
                  (ii)  OWN-KEY DISCIPLINE -- every recovered bundle decrypts
                        only with the wallet's own seed-derived K_tx keys
                        (A8-A11 CanDecrypt); recovery never credits a coin
                        addressed to someone else.
                  (iii) SOUND ACCUMULATOR -- the recovered set contains only
                        genuine own-bundles (no forged candidate is ever
                        silently admitted): recovered \subseteq RealBundles.
                  Result: VERIFIED -- UNBOUNDED, via inductive invariant.

                LIVENESS half (availability) -- conditional recovery liveness:
                  under fairness, if the wallet is recovering and a genuine
                  own-bundle is held by >= 1 reachable honest replica that is
                  queried, the bundle is EVENTUALLY recovered.
                  Result: NOT in the unbounded certificate. The genuine
                  fairness-conditional temporal property is UNCHECKABLE on
                  Apalache 0.58.0 ("Handling fairness is not supported yet").
                  The certificate instead carries the ENABLEDNESS SURROGATE
                  RecoverEnabledForHeld (a held real bundle is always

                  MEDIUM-liveness label. See notes.md for the scope decision.

What "unbounded" means here (SAFETY half only)
----------------------------------------------
The recovery state machine interleaves the recovery layer (Recover / Finish)
with the underlying Transport actions; the reachable state space is unbounded
(Transport's Retry increments an attempt nonce without bound). This proof
removes the execution-length bound for the SAFETY half: the conjunction of
checks [2] (base), [3] (step) and [4] (implication) is a standard
inductive-invariant argument, so INV_P9_safety holds in EVERY reachable state,
for an arbitrary number of Recover / Finish / Publish / Replicate /
RecipientSigns / Ack / Retry / Drop transitions. [1] is a bounded reachability
sanity pass.

The proof instance fixes finite parameters via ConstInit (Transport K=3,
Holders={1,2,3}, RecipientOpPk=10, IvkHolders=KtxHolders={20,21}, Eve=99;
recovery WalletId=30, Bundles={1,2,8,9}, RealBundles={1,2},
AddressedForgeries={8}) and a finite nonce universe NonceU=0..3 for the START
state of the step check. The inductive argument is uniform in these sizes:
Recover only ever unions in gate-accepted candidates (exactly RealBundles by
the oracle definitions), and every other action leaves `recovered` unchanged.
Confirmed by re-running [2]/[3]/[4] at a larger instance (Holders={1,2,3,4},
Bundles={1,2,3,7,8,9}, RealBundles={1,2,3}, AddressedForgeries={7,8}) -- all
three NoError (see notes.md).

The recovery layer (design)
---------------------------
property.tla EXTENDS Transport and adds a recovery layer over Sec. 4.5:
  - Bundle universe split into RealBundles (genuine own-bundles, verify against
    Bitcoin AND decrypt with WalletId), AddressedForgeries (decryptable by the
    wallet but their recursive proof does NOT verify -- the genuine step-4
    threat), and unaddressed forgeries (neither).
  - AcceptsBundle(b) is the Sec. 4.5 step-4 gate: KnowledgeSound(bVerifies(b),
    bStmtHolds(b)) /\ bVerifies(b) /\ CanDecrypt(bKtxHolders(b), WalletId).
  - Recover (Sec. 4.5 steps 3-4) pulls a NON-DETERMINISTIC candidate set Q from
    the untrusted network (any subset of Bundles: it may withhold real bundles
    and/or inject forgeries) and unions ONLY the gate-accepted members into the
    accumulator `recovered`. Forged / non-own candidates are discarded.

The inductive invariant
-----------------------
  IndInv == TrTypeOK /\ RecTypeOK /\ INV_P9_safety
where RecTypeOK == recPhase \in {"recovering","done"} /\ recovered \subseteq
Bundles, and INV_P9_safety is the three-conjunct safety property above.
  - recovered \subseteq RealBundles is inductive on its own: Recover unions in
    only `{b \in Q : AcceptsBundle(b)}`, and AcceptsBundle(b) implies
    bVerifies(b) i.e. b \in RealBundles; Finish/TrNext leave `recovered`
    unchanged.
  - (i) and (ii) then follow because every member of RealBundles satisfies both
    AcceptsBundle and CanDecrypt(., WalletId).
  - TrTypeOK supplies the finite Transport state-bounding facts; RecTypeOK the
    recovery ones. No further strengthening was required.

IndInvInit is the assignment-form restatement of IndInv used as the --init
predicate of the step check (Apalache requires each variable ASSIGNED, not
merely constrained). It assigns each Transport variable from its finite type
domain (reusing P08's form) plus recPhase and recovered, then asserts IndInv;
it is logically equivalent to IndInv.

Checks [2] + [3] + [4] together are the unbounded SAFETY proof. [E] is the
liveness surrogate. [L] is the honest attempt at the genuine temporal property.

The liveness-handling decision (honest scope)
---------------------------------------------
The genuine conditional-liveness property ConditionalLiveness is
  WF_vars(Recover) => <>[]( \A b \in RealBundles : b \in recovered )
i.e. under weak fairness of Recover, every genuine own-bundle is eventually
recovered. Apalache 0.58.0 CANNOT check this: the temporal encoder throws
"scala.NotImplementedError: Handling fairness is not supported yet!" (check [L],
transcript below). This is expected and is reported AS EXPECTED by verify.sh.

To confirm the property genuinely NEEDS fairness (and is not vacuously true), we
also ran the fairness-FREE form <>[](all recovered) with --temporal (encodable
by Apalache): it returns a COUNTEREXAMPLE -- without fairness the untrusted
network can withhold forever (a pure stutter/withholding trace), so the bundle
is never recovered. That is exactly the audit's "if all collude, the user is
stuck": liveness is genuinely availability-conditional, which is why it is not a
safety property and not part of this certificate.

The certificate therefore carries [E] RecoverEnabledForHeld -- a SAFETY
(state) invariant: while recovering, for every genuine own-bundle there exists a
query set whose gate output contains it, i.e. the Recover action is always ABLE
to bring a held real bundle in. This is honestly WEAKER than temporal liveness:
it certifies "recovery can always make progress on a held real bundle", NOT
"it eventually does" (which needs the fairness Apalache cannot encode). Full
temporal liveness is OUT of this certificate's scope, matching Pass-3's
MEDIUM-liveness label.

Vacuity probe and negative control (the model earns its keep)
------------------------------------------------------------
(V) Reachability / vacuity probe. Asserting "recovered stays empty" as an
    invariant over the reachable space
      apalache-mc check --cinit=ConstInit --init=Init --next=Next \
        --inv=RecoveredStaysEmpty --length=4 probe.tla
    yields Error at State 1 (recovered = {1} or {2}) -- recovery actually
    recovers something, so INV_P9_safety is not vacuously satisfied by an empty
    accumulator.

(NC) Negative control -- the verify gate is load-bearing. Delete the
    `bVerifies(b)` conjunct (the Sec. 4.5 step-4 verify gate) from AcceptsBundle
    in a /tmp copy and run
      apalache-mc check --cinit=ConstInit --init=Init --next=Next \
        --inv=INV_P9_safety --length=4 property.tla
    Outcome: Error -- state invariant 3 (recovered \subseteq RealBundles)
    violated at State 1. Trace: the network returns the ADDRESSED forgery 8
    (decryptable by the wallet, but its proof does not verify); without the
    verify gate the own-key check alone admits it -- a false-accept:
      State0: recovered = {},  recPhase = "recovering"
      State1: recovered = {8}, recPhase = "recovering"  (Recover admits forgery 8)
              => recovered \not\subseteq RealBundles  [false-accept]
    With the verify gate present (committed model), forgery 8 fails bVerifies and
    is discarded -- the false-accept is impossible. The unaddressed forgery 9 is
    rejected by BOTH gates. The committed Transport.tla and property.tla are
    unchanged; the /tmp copy and its _apalache-out were discarded.
