--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The chain-observer projection ChainView (the heart of P05)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ChainView(b) is what a passive chain-only observer reads off ONE admitted
BatchInscription: EXACTLY the five on-chain fields of the 231-byte inscription
(Sec. 3.5 byte layout):
    publisherPk        (offset 3,   32B, Pk_p, BIP-340 x-only)
    prevRoot           (offset 35,  32B, accumulator root)
    newRoot            (offset 67,  32B, accumulator root)
    bundleLocator      (offset 99,  32B, Hc over the BatchBundle)
    blockAnchorHeight  (offset 163,  4B, big-endian u32 height)
It DROPS the Onchain record's batchNullifiers, memberValidities and anchorOk:
post-#40 those are BUNDLE-/admission-internal -- the SpendRecords, member
nullifiers, and AggregateBatchProof live OFF-CHAIN inside the BatchBundle
(Sec. 3.1, Sec. 4.6); anchorOk is the verifier's own §3.5 scan outcome, not an
inscribed byte. This split is the post-#40 on-chain reality the projection
encodes: the footprint is CONSTANT-SIZE and carries no per-member structure.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DYNAMIC vs STRUCTURAL vs AXIOM (the honest split)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DYNAMIC (machine-checked, negatively controllable):
  PublisherOnlyLink (INV_P5 conjunct 1). For every admitted inscription, its
  ChainView.publisherPk is a publisher key AND never equals an account identity
  key (owner = address = H(Pk0), or current_pubkey = rotating Pk_i) of any
  account behind any on-chain nullifier. This is the §3.5 normative claim "the
  publisher identity is the only on-chain link", machine-checked. It is genuinely
  dynamic (relates Admit-produced publisherPk to Admit-committed account keys
  across the whole history) and negatively controllable (deleting the
  publisher/account namespace separation breaks it -- see negative control).
  Proven UNBOUNDED via the set-projection inductive invariant:
    [P2] base   OnInit => IndInv_P5
    [P3] step   IndInv_P5 /\ OnNext => IndInv_P5'   (OnNext = PublisherSign
                                                     \/ Admit \/ Confirm \/ Reorg)
    [P4] impl   IndInv_P5 => ObservableGuarantee
  -> the load-bearing fact (no committed nullifier's account key is a publisher
     key) holds in every reachable state, any number of transitions.

STRUCTURAL (by-construction, clearly labelled -- NOT a tautology dressed as
dynamic; the P10 vacuity-defect discipline is applied here deliberately):
  NoMemberStructureOnChain (INV_P5 conjunct 2). The observable projection of
  every admitted inscription carries EXACTLY the five on-chain fields and NO
  bundle-only field (batchNullifiers / memberValidities absent from DOMAIN
  ChainView). This is a property of the PROJECTION FUNCTION, true by
  construction; it is checked over the reachable space ([P5], length 6) only to
  confirm the projection is well-formed on every admitted inscription. It is
  explicitly labelled structural -- it is not exercised by the transition
  relation and is not the load-bearing dynamic claim.

AXIOM (quoted, scoped, NOT claimed verified):
  - A3 (root / nullifier preimage-resistance). The model represents a root AS
    the SET of nullifiers it commits to (A16/RootCommitsSet); on the WIRE roots
    are 32-byte Poseidon/SMT digests whose preimage is hidden by A3. The
    set-representation IS the A16/A3 boundary: everything the dynamic invariant
    reads off the committed set is, on the wire, hidden behind A3.
  - BIP-32-PRF (rotating-key unlinkability). Each transition uses a fresh
    hardened-derived Pk_i (Sec. 1.2); without the seed no key path links two
    transitions. The PRF property of the BIP-32 KDF is the axiom; the model does
    not re-derive it.
  - A2 (nk-secrecy). nf = Hc("Nullifier", nk || coin.identifier); without nk the
    nf is indistinguishable from a random 256-bit digest (Pass-3 P5).
  - Network-layer hygiene (Pass-3 P5 MEDIUM half): publisher funding/UTXO
    exposure (§3.8 broadcaster-paid is MAY, not MUST), NIP-44/NIP-59
    cross-protocol fingerprinting, relay choice, Tor, long-run intersection
    attacks. These are operational, depend on user behaviour the spec cannot
    enforce, and are OUT OF SCOPE here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"UNBOUNDED" and the Seq variable (honest scope -- same situation as P02)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PublisherOnlyLink and NoMemberStructureOnChain both quantify over DOMAIN
onAdmittedChain. Apalache encodes a Seq with a STATIC capacity, so an inductive
step that assumes-and-reestablishes a chain-quantified property of ARBITRARY
length is not one unbounded SMT query (exactly the situation P02 documents for
its prev_root continuity). We are explicit, mirroring P02's certificate:

  - The DYNAMIC claim is certified UNBOUNDED through its SET-PROJECTION inductive
    invariant IndInv_P5, whose load-bearing conjunct is the STATE fact "no
    committed nullifier (onPending \cup onCompleted) has an account key in the
    publisher namespace". This is preserved by every OnNext action: Admit only
    adds nullifiers drawn from Nullifiers (whose account keys are the account
    namespace, disjoint from Publishers by construction, Sec. 1.2); Confirm/
    Reorg move nullifiers between/out of the zones; PublisherSign touches no
    nullifier. The inductive step [P3] uses an assignment-form init (IndInvInit)
    that ASSIGNS every Onchain variable, drawing each set from its powerset and
    Gen-constructing the sequence (capacity <= 4). Because IndInv_P5 never reads
    the chain and the set effects of OnNext do not depend on the chain's value,
    the step is a faithful statement about the SET PROJECTION; the Gen capacity
    bounds only the bookkeeping witness, never the conclusion.
  - The full history-quantified forms INV_P5 ([P1]) and the structural
    projection fact ([P5]) are certified BOUNDED (length 6 = finality depth K).

Vacuity probe (the dynamic invariant is asserted over a POPULATED set)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A throwaway probe.tla EXTENDS property and asserts
  NoAccountKeysEverOnChain == AccountKeysOnChain = {}
checked with
  apalache-mc check --cinit=P5ConstInit --init=OnInit --next=OnNext \
    --inv=NoAccountKeysEverOnChain --length=3 probe.tla
yields The outcome is: Error (exit 12): the reachable space DOES contain admitted
inscriptions whose committed nullifiers carry account identity keys (a non-empty
AccountKeysOnChain). PublisherOnlyLink therefore asserts the publisher/account-
key disjointness over a NON-EMPTY account-key set, not vacuously over {}. The
probe and its _apalache-out were discarded; the committed model is unchanged.

Negative control on the FULL model (the namespace separation is load-bearing)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A /tmp copy of property.tla (module `propertyNC`) adds a const init NCConstInit
that builds the smoke nullifiers over an account whose identity key COLLIDES with
a publisher key:
  NCCoinId(i) == MkCoinId(CanonicalEmptyAccount(1, 1), MkAssetId(...), i)
  NCConstInit == Nullifiers = { MkNullifier(0, NCCoinId(i)) : i \in 1..3 }
              /\ Publishers = 1..2 /\ BundleLocators = 1..2
i.e. owner = current_pubkey = 1, and 1 \in Publishers -- the §1.2 publisher/
account namespace separation is DELETED. Checked with
  apalache-mc check --cinit=NCConstInit --init=OnInit --next=OnNext \
    --inv=INV_P5 --length=3 propertyNC.tla
yields The outcome is: Error (exit 12): PublisherOnlyLink (INV_P5 conjunct,
state invariant 0) VIOLATED at State 2. Trace:
  State0  empty chain.
  State1  PublisherSign            -- publisher pk=1 authorises the batch.
  State2  Admit  publisherPk |-> 1 -- an inscription is admitted whose committed
                                      nullifier carries owner |-> 1, currentPk
                                      |-> 1; publisherPk (1) == an account
                                      identity key (1) => PublisherOnlyLink
                                      violated. A chain observer reading the
                                      publisher field would read an account key.
With the namespace separation present (committed P5ConstInit: account key 0,
publishers {1,2}), all six verify.sh checks return NoError. The /tmp copy and its
_apalache-out were discarded; module/Onchain.tla and the committed property.tla
are unchanged.

POST-#40 DELTA (see notes.md for the full paragraph). Pass-3 was written PRE-#40:
it states the on-chain observer learns k_j (the accepted input-count leak), the
rotating Pk_i, and the spent nf's from a per-record SpendRecord on-chain surface.
Post-#40 that surface NO LONGER EXISTS: SpendRecords/nullifiers/k_j moved
OFF-CHAIN into the BatchBundle, and the chain footprint became the constant-size
BatchInscription. The redesign STRENGTHENS P5 -- the accepted k_j leak is gone
from the chain, and the publisher pubkey is a NEW on-chain datum, which is
exactly what PublisherOnlyLink machine-checks as the sole (non-account) on-chain
link. The HIGH on-chain bound is therefore confirmed AND strengthened.
