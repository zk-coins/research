records. This proof removes the execution-length bound: the conjunction of
checks [2] (base), [3] (step) and [4] (implication) is a standard inductive-
invariant argument, so NoDoubleSpend holds in EVERY reachable state, for an
arbitrary number of Admit/Confirm/Reorg transitions. The nullifier universe is

local and uniform in |Nullifier| -- confirmed by re-running [2]/[3]/[4] at
|Nullifier| = 12 (see notes.md). Data-domain finiteness per state is intrinsic
to SMT-based model checking and is recorded as a modelling parameter, not a
hidden bound on the number of transitions.
